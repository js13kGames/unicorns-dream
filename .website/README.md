🦄 Unicorn’s Dream
This is the dream of every unicorn: to ride the rainbow across the stars. Help this one ride his!
To survive the cosmic highway, our unicorn must collect as many magical mushrooms as possible. If he misses them, the vibrant colors of the rainbow bridge begin to dissolve and fade away, leading the unicorn to fall.

Dash, leap, and steer across 7 challenging levels. Every level you conquer completely restores the rainbow for the next lvl.

🕹️ Controls:
Steer Left / Right: Mouse movements or ← / → Arrow Keys
Jump: Spacebar or Mouse Click
Dash: E key 

PS: This game is inspired by the classic favorite, Snail Mail.
